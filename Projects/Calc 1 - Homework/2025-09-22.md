Using $f'(x) = \lim_{z \to x}\frac{f(z)-f(x)}{z-x}$ and $f(x)=x^2-1$

1. Find $f'(x)$
	$$\begin{aligned}
	&=\quad \lim_{z \to x}\frac{(z^2-1)-(x^2-1)}{z-x} \\\\
	&\text{Difference of squares}\\
	&=\quad \lim_{z \to x}\frac{z^2-x^2}{z-x} \quad=\quad \lim_{z \to x}\frac{(z+x)(x-z)}{z-x} \\\\
	&=\quad \lim_{z \to x}\frac{(z+x)\cancel{(x-z)}}{\cancel{z-x}} \quad = \quad \lim_{z \to x}z+x \\\\
	&=\quad \boxed{2x}
	\end{aligned}$$
