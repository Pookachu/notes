Find the slope of the following curve at $x= 8$.
$y={1\over x-3}$
$$\begin{gather}
\lim_{h\to0}\frac{\frac{1}{(x+h)-3}-\frac{1}{x-3}}{h} \\
= \lim_{h\to0}\frac{\frac{x-3}{(x+h-3)(x-3)}-\frac{x+h-3}{(x+h-3)(x-3)}}{h} \\
=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{\frac{-h}{(x+h-3)(x-3)}}{h} = -\frac{1}{x^2-6x+9}
\end{gather}$$
