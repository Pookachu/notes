Question 4:
Find the slope of the graph of the function $f(x)= \sqrt{10x}$ at $(10,10)$ then find an equation of the tangent to the graph at that point.
$$\begin{gather}
\lim_{h\to0}\frac{\sqrt{10(10+h)}-\sqrt{10(10)}}{h} \\
= \lim_{h\to0}\frac{(\sqrt{100+10h)}-\sqrt{100})(\sqrt{100+10h}+\sqrt{100})}{h(\sqrt{100+10h}+\sqrt{100})} \\
= \lim_{h\to0}\frac{10\cancel h}{\cancel h(\sqrt{100+10h}+\sqrt{100})} \\
= \lim_{h\to0} \frac{10}{\sqrt{100+10h}+\sqrt{100}} = \frac{10}{20} = \boxed{1\over 2} \\\\
y={1\over2}(x-10)-10
\end{gather}$$
