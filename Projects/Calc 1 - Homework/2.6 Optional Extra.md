Question 5:
$$\begin{aligned}
	&f(x) = {7x^4+5 \over x^4-x^3+x+8} \\\\
	
	\text{a. }& \lim_{x\to\infty}({7x^4+5\over x^4-x^3+x+8}) \\\\
	
	&= \quad \lim_{x\to\infty} (\frac{\frac{7x^4}{x^4}+\frac{5}{x^4}}{\frac{x^4}{x^4}-\frac{x^3}{x^4}+\frac{x}{x^4}+\frac{8}{x^4}}) \\\\
	
	&= \quad \lim_{x\to\infty} (\frac{7+\frac{5}{x^4}}{1-\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{x^3}+\frac{8}{x^4}}) \\\\
	
	&= \quad \frac{7}{1}\quad=\quad \boxed{7} \\\\\\
	
	\text{b. }& \lim_{x\to-\infty}({7x^4+5\over x^4-x^3+x+8}) = \boxed{7}
\end{aligned}$$
