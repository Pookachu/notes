Question 3:

Find the slope of the following curve at $x=6$
$y=\frac{1}{x-1}$

Solution: [[Chain Rule]]
$$\begin{gather} 
y=h(x)=g(f(x)) \\
h'(x) = f'(g(x))\cdot g'(x) \\\\
g(x) = \frac{1}{x}, \quad g'(x) = -{1\over x^2}\\ 
f(x) = 1-x,\quad f'(x) = -1 \\\\
h'(x) = -({1\over x})\cdot -{1\over x^2}
\end{gather}$$
