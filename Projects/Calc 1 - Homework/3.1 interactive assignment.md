Question 3:

Find the slope of the following curve at $x=6$
$y=\frac{1}{x-1}$

Solution: [[Chain Rule]]
$$\begin{gather} 
y=h(x)=g(f(x)) \\
h'(x) = g'(f(x))\cdot g'(x) \\\\
f(x) = x-1, \quad g(u) = {1\over u}\\
f'(x) = 1, \quad g'(u)= -\frac{1}{u^2} \\\\
f'(g(x)) = -1\cdot -\frac{1}{(x-1)^2} \\
\to -\frac{1}{(x-1)^2}\cdot(1) = -\frac{1}{(x-1)^2} \\\\
\to h'(6) = -\frac{1}{(6-1)^2}= \boxed{-\frac{1}{25}}
\end{gather}$$



Question 4:

Find an equation for the tangent line to the curve at the given point. Then sketch the curve and tangent line together.
$y=7-x^2,\quad (3,-2)$

Equation for the tangent line: $$\begin{gather}
f'(x)=-2x \\
f'(3) = -6 \\
y-(-2)=-6(x-3) = y+2=-6x+18 \\
y= -6x+16
\end{gather}$$

Question 5:

An object is dropped from the top of a cliff $660$ meters high. Its height above the ground $t$ seconds after it is dropped is $660 - 4.9t^2$ determine its speed 6 seconds after it is dropped.

$$f(t) = 660 -4.9t^2,\quad f'(t)=-9.8t, \quad f'(6)= -9.8(6)= -58.8$$
